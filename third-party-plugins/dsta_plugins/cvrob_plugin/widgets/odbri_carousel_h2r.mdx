  <h3>Brittle Misclassified Images (Object Detection)</h3>

  <span>
    This widget shows images from your dataset where that are the most brittle, sorted in descending order of brittleness. “Brittle” images are
    those where the model's confidence, averaged across all images, drops the most under the specified corruption
    algorithm and severity interval. An image’s brittleness is defined as the magnitude
    of this confidence drop.
  </span>

  <span>
    Compared to the static image display, this view is designed for closer inspection
    of individual images, making it easier to examine how and why each sample fails
    under perturbation.
  </span>
</div>

  <h4>
    <b>How to read?</b>
  </h4>

  <p>
    The widget compares each image before and after applying the chosen corruption severity.
  </p>

  <p>
    The left column shows the original (or lower severity) version of each image.
    The right column shows the same image after applying higher severity corruption.
  </p>

  <p><b>Legend:</b></p>

  <ul>
    <li>image: file path or identifier of the image</li>
    <li>ground truth objects: number of objects in the image (that are of a class the model is trained to detect)</li>
    <li>detections: number of objects detected by the model within the image</li>
    <li>top confidence: max confideence of a given detection in the image</li>
    <li>pred_proba: model confidence (probability) for the predicted class</li>
    <li>brittleness: brittleness score, a consolidated averaged measure of the drop in confidence of the detections within the image</li>
  </ul>
</div>
